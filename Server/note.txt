Tier 3 Weight: Base 0. Starts appearing when Luck > 10. Grows fast.

🔥 SKILL SYSTEM CHEAT SHEET (DO NOT FORGET!) 🔥
=========================================================

1. THE 3 TIMERS (Strict Order)
   • CastTime: The wind-up. Locks the player in "Casting" state.
   • ChannelDuration: The active spell time (e.g., firing a laser, maintaining an aura). 
   • Cooldown: Starts ticking ONLY AFTER the Channel Duration hits 0.

2. QUEUED VS INDEPENDENT
   • Standard Skills: Wait in a round-robin queue. Only cast when 100% idle.
   • "isIndependent": true -> Bypasses the queue. Casts in the background instantly (e.g., Drones). Does NOT lock the player's casting bar.

3. LIFESPAN VS DURATION
   • ChannelDurationSeconds = Belongs to the CASTER (how long they hold the spell).
   • Lifespan = Belongs to the PROJECTILE (how long it exists).
   • Rule of Thumb: Delete "lifespan" from your projectile JSON! The C# engine auto-calculates it perfectly (Range/Speed for bullets, ChannelDuration for orbitals). Only use "lifespan" if you want to force an override.

4. DAMAGE TYPE
   • Skill "baseDamage" is ALWAYS treated as Magic Damage! It scales with the "magicDamage" stat.

5. TARGETING & SHOP PHASE
   • Ceasefire: Auto-skills will NEVER fire during the Shop Phase.
   • "ClosestEnemy": If a skill targets the closest enemy during the Shop Phase (or when the map is empty), it will SILENTLY FAIL. 
   • Orbitals/Auras: Always use "targetType": "Self" so they spawn on you regardless of enemies.



=========================================================
💀 DIFFICULTY SYSTEM CHEAT SHEET 💀
=========================================================

1. WHERE TO EDIT
   • All core math is controlled strictly via "Configs/Difficulties.json". 
   • Do not hardcode scaling in C#!

2. ENEMY STAT SCALING
   • "EnemyBaseScaling": 0.025 means enemies gain +2.5% Max HP and Damage per wave. 

3. SPAWN RATE ENGINE (The Ramp-Up)
   • "InitialSpawnDelay": How slow enemies spawn on Wave 1 (e.g., 2.0s).
   • "DelayDecreasePerWave": How much time is subtracted from the delay every wave (e.g., 0.1s).
   • "MinimumSpawnDelay": The absolute floor so the game doesn't crash (e.g., 0.1s).
   • NOTE: The final delay is ALWAYS divided by the number of players. More players = exponentially faster spawns!

4. ECONOMY & PUNISHMENT
   • "GoldMultiplier" / "XpMultiplier": Directly multiplies the value of drops when picked up.
   • "BaseRerollCost": The starting reroll price on Wave 1.
   • "RerollCostIncreasePerWave": How much the STARTING price inflates per wave.
   • "RerollInflation": How much the price jumps EVERY TIME the player clicks the Reroll button in a single shop phase.

5. EXCLUSIVE ENEMIES (Enemies.json)
   • "AllowedDifficulties": ["hard"] -> This enemy will ONLY spawn on Hard mode.
   • "AllowedDifficulties": [] -> (Empty list) This enemy spawns on all difficulties.


=========================================================
📈 STAT SCALING & MATH CHEAT SHEET 📈
=========================================================

1. THE TWO STAT BUCKETS (How to assign stats in JSON)
   • "statBonuses": The FLAT bucket (+). Use this to add raw numbers (e.g., +5 Armor, +20 Ranged Damage).
     *CRITICAL*: This is ALSO where you place Global percentage stats like "damagePercent" or "xpGainPercent" (e.g., "damagePercent": 0.20 grants exactly +20% global damage).
   • "statMultipliers": The PERCENTAGE bucket (%). Use this to scale a character's entire flat stat pool. 
     *Rule*: These stack ADDITIVELY. (e.g., Equipping two items with "armor": 0.50 gives +100% total Armor scale).

2. GLOBAL DAMAGE vs. WEAPON SCALING
   • "damageScaling" (Weapon Property): Determines how much a specific weapon feeds on your character's stats. 
     (e.g., "rangedDamage": 1.2 -> The bullet takes 120% of your Ranged Damage stat).
   • "damagePercent" (Character Stat): The GLOBAL multiplier applied to the final bullet damage at the very end of the math loop.

3. COMMON EXAMPLES (How to write the JSON)
   • Give a character +20% Ranged Damage globally:
     Put "rangedDamage": 0.20 inside "statMultipliers".
   • Give an item +10% Global Damage to ALL weapons:
     Put "damagePercent": 0.10 inside "statBonuses".
   • Give exactly +5 flat Magic Damage:
     Put "magicDamage": 5 inside "statBonuses".
   • Wipe out a character's Magic Damage completely (-100%):
     Put "magicDamage": -1.0 inside "statMultipliers".
